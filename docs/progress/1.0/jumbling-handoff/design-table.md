## Observation log

### [2026-10-18] Census and stage 12 measurements that bear on the design

1. **DO-01.** At the cap where the census rule found its repair, the repair list held 1 to 61 pairs (median 5) over all 63 repair steps of the seed 7101 record (depth 40): 15 to 61 (median 27) at the 11 steps before step 44, and 1 to 59 (median 3) from step 44 on, with a single pair at 15 of those 52 steps and at most 12 at 48. Evidence: `research/jumbling/restoration/11-scramble-census/README.md:203-206`, `research/jumbling/restoration/11-scramble-census/README.md:257-260`. For this line: the list must read well from one row to about sixty, and in the tail it is usually a few rows. It is one record, not a bound (`research/jumbling/restoration/11-scramble-census/README.md:375-377`).
2. **DO-02.** A person who names caps by decreasing N_c gets an empty list at every cap before the right one. The right cap was the first named at 15 of the 63 steps, the second at 26, the third at 13, and at most the 28th. Evidence: `research/jumbling/restoration/11-scramble-census/README.md:254-257`. For this line: naming a cap and reading an empty list is the usual case, so naming must be one quick action, and an empty list must read as an answer (`docs/wiki/decisions/owner-decisions-2026-10-17-repair-pairs.md:51-52`).
3. **DO-03.** Without the list, every step is a search: a face match took a median of 71 checks (at most 1,004), ruling one out a median of 531 (at most 1,239), and a tail repair a median of about 110 openings and 800 pairs, each pair with its own checks for y and z. Evidence: `research/jumbling/restoration/11-scramble-census/README.md:185-202`. For this line: checking a proposed twist (turn preview, then the exact check on release: `docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-observation.md:119`) is the descent's most repeated action and needs a short keyboard path. Whether reading the domains cuts these counts was not measured (`research/jumbling/restoration/11-scramble-census/README.md:191-193`).
4. **DO-04.** Nothing visible marks the lowering pair: d passes the face match after the opening at 6 of the 63 steps, c covers d at 30 and d covers c at 26. At every repair step a median of 13 caps were above their floor with no single twist that lowers N. Evidence: `research/jumbling/restoration/11-scramble-census/README.md:207-215`. For this line: the floor shows only where no single twist helps. A cap above its floor is no sign that one does, and the view must not present it as one (rule 6: `docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-observation.md:42`).
5. **DO-05.** The twists are few and the finding is slow: 199 twists put back 98.5% of the 40-twist scramble of seed 7101. Evidence: `research/jumbling/restoration/11-scramble-census/README.md:107-109`. For this line: Jumble screens serve reading and checking more than twisting, as in the retained table, whose commit path is narrow (`docs/progress/1.0/command-table.md:61`).
