## Observation log

### [2026-10-18] What the restoration research found that the engine must respect

1. **EO-01.** R(I_a) is infinite: an explicit family of words F_t, admissible from solved, takes seven pieces to left height at least 3888 + 132t, and no uniform bound on the length of one clean step exists. Evidence: `research/jumbling/restoration/10-x1-unbounded/README.md:8-9`, `:22-25`. For this line: by the argument of `docs/progress/1.0/packets/engineering/ENG-0J-jumbling-backend.md:76` (only finitely many matrices over Q(√5) have entries of bounded height), pose entries grow without bound under I_a; the pose table and the store get no size bound from the menu, and no fixed limit on macro length follows from the theory.

2. **EO-02.** N changes only along the cut of the cap that is twisted, and N = 0 exactly on the lattice (both proved). Evidence: `research/jumbling/theory/restoration.md:65`, `:69`; `research/jumbling/manual/02-descent.md:143-145`. For this line: the N change of a proposed word is a sum of one-cap changes along its prefixes, so the per-revision rim summary of 600 rows (`docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-observation.md:173`) carries the cap map and every N check.

3. **EO-03.** On the seed 7101 record (depth 40, 63 repair steps) the repair list at the cap where a repair was found held 1 to 61 pairs, a median of 5; from step 44 on, 1 to 59, a median of 3, which is 0.08% to 6.67% of the 118 to 1,593 pairs at that cap, with a single pair at 15 of 52 steps. Naming caps by decreasing N_c reached that cap first or second at 41 of the 63 steps, and at most 28th. The research script tries every pair at a cap, 3 to 5 minutes per cap. Only this record was measured. Evidence: `research/jumbling/restoration/11-scramble-census/README.md:203-206`, `:253-260`; `docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-observation.md:176`. For this line: the list is short, but each request is a full search over the cap's pairs on one revision, and a step can take many requests; the minutes are research timings, not performance evidence.

4. **EO-04.** In the research list, y and z are the twists that leave the lowest N_d and N_c, ties going to the smaller menu index, and no pair is formed when d or c is blocked or already closed, or when no twist of d lowers N_d. The owner's reading says "the best twist" and gives no tie rule. Evidence: `research/jumbling/restoration/11-scramble-census/scripts/density.py:21-40`; `docs/wiki/decisions/owner-decisions-2026-10-17-repair-pairs.md:48-50`. For this line: the interface proposal must state one tie rule; with another rule the product lists can differ from the measured ones.

5. **EO-05.** The research cover test places pieces by float centroids of their exact poses, while rule 2 of the observation requirements has the engine decide every geometric predicate exactly. Evidence: `research/jumbling/theory/seams.py:35-36`, `:83-91`, `:174`; `docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-observation.md:34`. For this line: a covers check needs an exact definition before it is offered; the census's 7 cover steps were not measured for a person (`research/jumbling/restoration/11-scramble-census/README.md:193-194`).

6. **EO-06.** Steps found at the depth 40 stop run from 10 to 1,158 letters: strict′ steps of 36 to 84 letters for ten units, closures of 222, 270 and 704 letters, the eight steps so far of the second driver run with 10 to 1,130 letters, and the solver's T1′ steps of 160 to 1,158 letters, re-checked by the integrator. The 36 corrections have 5 to 5,629 twists, and the 35 seeds 7,024 together, kept as 70 stored macros with their inverses. Evidence: `research/jumbling/restoration/12-low-height/README.md:483-484`, `:488-490`, `:532-534`, `:597-600`; `research/jumbling/manual/04-lattice.md:139-141`, `:245-282`. For this line: stored words of thousands of single twists are normal input for the store, the replay and the preview.

7. **EO-07.** Steps computed independently at one state need not compose: after b12's strict′ step, b14's 60-letter census word, computed at the stop, is still admissible but takes 17 lattice pieces off. Used unchanged, 52 of the 156 ordered pairs of thirteen steps computed at the stop fail, and they compose at most six deep; a step stays strict′ after another exactly when the other's landing leaves the first step's complete Bad set vacant. Two of the driver's steps changed the poses of other units: clean steps, but not strict′ ones. Evidence: `research/jumbling/restoration/12-low-height/README.md:604-606`, `:584-587`, `:490-491`. For this line: admissibility at every prefix does not show that a stored macro still does what it did; the preview at the current revision must show lattice pieces taken off and off pieces whose pose changes (EH-15).

8. **EO-08.** Existence is decided by finite exact tests without building a word: N1 and N2 are necessary, and with a Jordan certificate for every orbit that meets the target they are sufficient (Corollary A1). b19's vacating word exists this way; a construction bound puts b19's step under 10^540 letters, and no such word is built. Evidence: `research/jumbling/theory/buffer-transport.md:17-19`, `:25`, `:37`; `research/jumbling/restoration/12-low-height/README.md:484`, `:581-583`. For this line: a proved existence gives no word to store or replay; the program replays only words the solver has, and showing an existence test is an owner question (EH-17).

9. **EO-09.** On the lattice ν has 45 coordinates. Every retained twist keeps ν = 0, and retained twists solve every reachable lattice configuration with ν = 0 (certificate: the retained group is the kernel of ν). After a jumbled history ν can take every value of C2^34 × C5^2. A correction is a fixed word of jumble and retained twists that acts the same from every lattice configuration, and ν adds. Evidence: `research/jumbling/manual/04-lattice.md:45-50`, `:60-66`, `:78-88`. For this line: ν comes from the labelled projection (reference: `research/jumbling/restoration/03-retained-group-and-lattice-restorer/scripts/nucoords.py:1`); a correction's ν change is fixed and checkable; corrections contain jumble twists, so they need Jumble on.

10. **EO-10.** ENG-0J has no macro for the jumbling puzzle, refuses the retained workflow's commands (macro, block tool, retained certificate) while Jumble is on, and allows Jumble off only at a witnessed retained-state checkpoint. J1 takes a witness from the caller or derives one from the journal only by merging consecutive twists of one grip and dropping identities; it never searches for one. A lattice state with ν ≠ 0 lies outside the retained group, so no retained word reaches it. Evidence: `docs/progress/1.0/packets/engineering/ENG-0J-jumbling-backend.md:20`, `:36-37`, `:74`; `research/jumbling/sim/README.md:62-66`, `:140`; `research/jumbling/manual/04-lattice.md:47-49`, `:60-61`. For this line: as written, ENG-0J leaves the lattice phase of a jumbled solve (corrections, then stars, setups and protection: `research/jumbling/manual/04-lattice.md:90-95`, `:125-153`) without stored macros or the retained tools; EH-14 and EH-18 close the gap.

11. **EO-11.** For the research, a configuration counts as restored when every sticker slot carries its home label; a piece may keep a rotation that fixes its stickers, which nothing on screen shows, and the lattice-phase theorem holds in that sense. That decision sets the research definition, not product behaviour. Contract equality and checkpoints use exact pose equality, and the labelled projection π is not injective. Evidence: `docs/wiki/decisions/owner-decisions-2026-10-15-jumbling-restored.md:21`, `:37`; `research/jumbling/manual/04-lattice.md:181`, `:185-186`; `research/jumbling/state-contract.md:146`. For this line: the engine keeps pose digests for replay and checkpoints and needs a separate sticker-level test; a session restored in the sticker sense need not be pose-equal to solved, which matters for Jumble off (EH-18).

12. **EO-12.** The census scramble is k admissible jumble twists at random grips with random items, a blocked draw drawn again, and no retained twists. In the random walks behind the W-J fixtures, 93% to 96% of attempted twists were blocked once the state was scrambled. Evidence: `research/jumbling/restoration/11-scramble-census/README.md:17-19`; `docs/progress/1.0/packets/engineering/ENG-0J-jumbling-backend.md:46`. For this line: a product scramble draws mostly blocked twists, so it runs as a job, and its record is stored apart from the solver's own twists (EH-19).

13. **EO-13.** The inverse of an admissible twist is admissible with the same inside set, so undo is always legal, and consecutive twists of one grip compose (both proved). Evidence: `docs/progress/1.0/packets/engineering/ENG-0J-jumbling-backend.md:64`, `:67`. For this line: a committed macro can be undone twist by twist in reverse without a blocked step, so its undo class can be `journal` (`docs/progress/1.0/command-table.md:44`).

14. **EO-14.** Off the lattice every unit has a clean step that an exhaustive search from the state finds (proved, length not bounded); a strict clean step at every state (gap M1) and coverage of the tail by a stated catalogue (B′) are open.
