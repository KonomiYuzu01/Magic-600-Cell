The view lab page is private and not in the repository; its only record is section 6.1 of the observation requirements (`docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-observation.md:136`; `research/jumbling/viewer/README.md:5`).

1. **LO-01.** In the 1.0 probe perspective, cell size varies about 12 times between the near and the far side, the far half collapses into the core, and a deep jumble reads as one blob. Evidence: `docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-observation.md:140`. For this line: this is the Look Lab's default projection (perspective, d4 = 1.18: `tools/looklab/README.md:308-309`); the view lab found it fit for the raw view, not for Global.
2. **LO-02.** Stereographic keeps angles, but sizes still vary strongly and the outer cells are very large. Evidence: `docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-observation.md:141`. For this line: the Look Lab already has this projection (`tools/looklab/README.md:232`); in the requirements it returns as the cap-centred raw-view preset (`docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-observation.md:101`).
3. **LO-03.** Two hemispheres show every cell once at moderate distortion, but neighbours across the equator land far apart. Evidence: `docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-observation.md:142`. For this line: a Global candidate whose cost is the split at the equator.
4. **LO-04.** Hopf rings draw every cell at the same size without distortion, so pieces and defects read cell by cell, and neighbours along a ring are face neighbours. The ring sheet is the most compact overview; its columns line up only roughly. Evidence: `docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-observation.md:143-144`. For this line: the Look Lab's projection parameter has no ring layout (`tools/looklab/README.md:232`), and the render contract's projection chain has no stage for one (`research/jumbling/render-contract.md:90-94`).
5. **LO-05.** The ring facts came from a scratch floating-point computation whose script was not kept. Evidence: `docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-observation.md:146`. For this line: no ring layout can be rebuilt from the repository yet. The Look Lab pins its own 20 rings of 30 cells and their graph (`tools/looklab/README.md:517-524`); whether these are the view lab's Hopf fibres is not stated (LH-05).
6. **LO-06.** With one hue per home cell (600 hues), neighbouring hues cannot be told apart. After 1,400 twists 17.5% of the stickers were still in their home cell, and stickers that only 2 to 5 caps can move cover about two thirds of a cell's area. Evidence: `docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-observation.md:153`. For this line: the research viewer's colouring by home facet (`research/jumbling/viewer/README.md:41`) is no model for Jumble. The Look Lab colours by ring class (`tools/looklab/README.md:97`), as the encoding rule asks: colour carries a class, never identity (`docs/wiki/decisions/owner-decisions-2026-10-02-design.md:41`).
7. **LO-07.** The owner's reference is Andrey Astrelin's MC7D, which folds the extra dimensions into each sticker so that the picture stays clean; the ring layouts apply the same idea to cells. Evidence: `docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-observation.md:151`. For this line: the owner's stated reference when the Global candidates are judged.
8. **LO-08.** Global perspective cannot show on which side of a cut a piece lies. The Local orthographic frame, which keeps the offset h exact on one axis, was the clearest view of a blocking reason. Evidence: `research/jumbling/viewer/README.md:243-245`. For this line: a Local for blocked caps needs an exact-offset axis. The Look Lab's Local frames the moving slots' rest anchors (`tools/looklab/README.md:737`), a different target.
9. **LO-09.** A blocked grip had 181 to 827 straddling pieces at S1 (median 345). The highlight read only when the other pieces dimmed, and one focus piece with a pager was needed to make one certificate legible. Evidence: `research/jumbling/viewer/README.md:227-229`. For this line: blocked-cap looks need a dimmed state and a single-focus state, close to the focus rule (inside the focus set every channel, outside only class colour: `docs/wiki/decisions/owner-decisions-2026-10-02-design.md:42`), while the never-hidden pieces stay visible (`docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-observation.md:37-41`).
10. **LO-10.** After the 10° twist, 3,789 of the 6,499 patch stickers had a vertex outside the 600-cell, the largest by 0.0225 facet distances against a cap depth of 0.032, and the world-fixed cage was needed to see the change at all. Evidence: `research/jumbling/viewer/README.md:220-223`. For this line: a jumbled shape needs a lattice reference (cage or nearest-lattice ghost) to be seen. Holes need exposed faces, which do not exist yet (LH-18).
11. **LO-11.** Grip status is undefined during motion, so the markers turn neutral, and both views carry an exact or preview badge. Evidence: `research/jumbling/viewer/README.md:102`, `:230-233`. For this line: every status mark needs a neutral look for motion, and the badge needs a look in both theme families, where status meanings are fixed (`docs/wiki/decisions/owner-decisions-2026-10-02-design.md:50`).
12. **LO-12.** The research full-state page has filters, an off-lattice tint, shrink, two projections, a blocked-grip list and the swept animation, but no domain tint, exposed faces, cap map, cut slice or checks. Evidence: `docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-observation.md:184-192`. For this line: Look Lab variants of these would be their first drawings.
13. **LO-13.** Random admissible scrambles leave about a third of the pieces on the lattice and under-test the views; the 360-twist deep scramble leaves 93.5% off. Domains seen so far: 6 at the I_a@40 stop, 18 at an S4@60 stop, and on the W-J I_a fixture path 37 at record 300 (more than 20 from record 82). Evidence: `docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-observation.md:84`, `:194`; `research/jumbling/theory/results/paths-I_a-300.json:1` (key `series.domains`). For this line: judge variants on the deep scramble, the W-J fixtures and the I_a@40 stop (LH-15, LH-16), and a domain palette on about 20 domains and on 37 (the Taste Lab log, TO-01). *Updated by LO-25 (2026-10-21).*
