## 1. Goal and acceptance
- Goal: rule go or no-go for each renderer candidate on the selection gate, exactly as recorded in `docs/wiki/decisions/renderer-candidates.md`, using only evidence from the owner's machine for the matching build.
- Acceptance: a ruling that states, per candidate (S-B, S-A2, S-D), **go** or **no-go**, citing for each the `renderer_gate.py` summary that decides it. Go only for a candidate whose W3 scene (all 259,800 sticker slots at full detail with the most complex animation) has the verdict `met`: three valid cold runs of one build identity, each with an average of at least 30 fps and p99 at most 33.3 ms over the full 180 s interval, the pooled frames meeting both as well, and the exact label check passing on every run. A near pass is a no-go. A predicted improvement is not evidence. VRAM above about 7 GB is ruled a recorded risk, not a no-go by itself.
- Non-goals: choosing the final renderer (E-2.4-05), design taste, NVIDIA features (never on this path).

## 2. Actual problem and reproduction
- The 12-day renderer window (stage days 3–14) has its go/no-go on window day 7. A no-go drops a candidate; if none passes, S-B continues as the dedicated renderer with a minimal shell and the risk is recorded (plan section 3).

## 3. Environment and versions
