Fill a copy of this packet under `work/reviews/packets/` (private); publish only the sanitized summary.

## 1. Goal and acceptance
- Goal: select the 1.0 renderer among the candidates that passed the day-7 go/no-go (E-2.4-04), or record that none passed.
- Acceptance:
  - The selected candidate's W5 scene (all 259,800 sticker slots at full detail; the design track's finalist preset H-02, motion table H-03, layout H-04 and visual features H-01) has verdict `met` in `renderer_gate.py`: three valid cold runs of one build, each and pooled with an average of at least 30 fps and p99 at most 33.3 ms over the full 180 s interval, the exact label check passing on every run. If no preset arrived, W3 is used and that is recorded.
  - Peak VRAM is recorded; above about 7 GB is a recorded risk.
  - Among candidates that pass, the choice prefers the one the vertical slice (stage days 13–15) can use with the least extra work, with the reasons stated.
  - A feature that alone breaks the gate is returned to the design track with its measured cost (H-06), not silently dropped.
  - If none passes, the failure is recorded with the evidence and the owner decides the next step (protocol section 5).
- Non-goals: NVIDIA enhancements (optional, off by default, evaluated later as add-ons), DLSS comparison sets (never part of the pass decision).
