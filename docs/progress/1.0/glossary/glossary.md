### <a id="cap-cover"></a>Cap cover

"d covers c": at [cap](#cap) c's cut, the face [pieces](#piece) whose centres lie in the region of a neighbouring cap d all sit on aligned [cut cells](#cut-cell), with the same number of them, at least ten, on each side of the cut. It hints that a later turn of d erased c's [seam](#seam) there, so that d was turned after c and comes off first. The hint can fail: other turns may have moved the overlap away, and the descent's techniques are no guarantee.

![Cap cover](diagrams/cap-cover.svg)

- First needed: L1 (Turning the puzzle)
- In the UI: not placed yet
- Source: EV5.18; DT-J1 M4; [manual sections 2.2 and 2.6](../../../../research/jumbling/manual/02-descent.md)
- Uses Cut cell before the manual introduces it: The chapter 2 introduction names the cover step before section 2.1 names cut cells; section 2.2, which defines covering, comes after both. Reviewed by the mathematician on 24 November 2026 (EV5.14).
- Review: The mathematician's pass of 23 November 2026 found M10 (the definition did not say what covering is) and their re-check M25 (the order is a hint that can fail: manual 2.2 and 2.6). Reviewed by the mathematician on 24 November 2026 (EV5.14): no finding.

### <a id="gauge"></a>Gauge

## Learning path (EV5.6)

The language learning path of EV5.6 (LP4-11): seven steps, each with the concepts it teaches, the command routes it exercises and one fixture task. `concepts` are EV5.6's lists with each phrase resolved to its glossary row; `closure` adds the rows those concepts' definitions use, and the rows the step's keys need (EV5.8), so every concept is taught before its step's task and before its keys open; `revisits` names a concept an earlier step taught; `waiting` names one the path cannot teach yet. `assumed` lists the terms the path takes from the manual (first use is the manual: owner, 23 November 2026) and from the solving glossary. Each fixture reads recorded instances only, at their recorded digests, and its answer is a value the learner reads or checks: no fixture chooses or outputs a word or a macro (the human-solve boundary). The mathematician reviewed the steps, their order, the fixtures and the keys on 24 November 2026 (EV5.14), and their findings are fixed; the human tests wait for the owner's form (EV5.12).
