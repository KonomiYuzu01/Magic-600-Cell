Status: **draft for stage 2.** Generated by `build.py` from `terms.json` and `learning-path.json`; edit the sources, not this page. One term per concept (charter M1). Each term enters at the learning level where it is first needed (charter M6, D7). The levels follow the steps of the [solving workflow](../solving-workflow.md) and are provisional until the owner decides the learning path; the UI locations are the planned surfaces of solving-workflow section 4, not a layout. Diagrams are rendered locally with the Mermaid CLI; sources and digests are in `diagrams/`. The language's entries come from chapter 04 section 6 of the language architecture (EV5.14, EV5.15, EV5.18); an entry marked for review waits for the mathematician.

Each fixture reads recorded instances only, at their recorded digests, and its answer is a value the learner reads or checks: no fixture chooses or outputs a word or a macro (the human-solve boundary).
