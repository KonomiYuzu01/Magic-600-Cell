# Owner decisions on ADR-D02: open research and the A4 picks

**How the decisions came about.**
- On 25 November 2026 the owner told the ADR line that open research is ADR-D02's core. The ADR line passed it to
  the ADR-D02 session, which re-ranked A4's options by it.
- The A4 pick page went to the owner on 25 November 2026 as one card, with twelve items to pick and the recommended
  option first ([plan](../../progress/1.0/adr-d02-plan.md)). Item 13, who builds the shells, was already settled
  ([25 November 2026](owner-decisions-2026-11-25.md), item 5).
- On 26 November 2026 the owner answered in the ADR-D02 session's chat, through a published copy of the page.

**What this page does not do.**
- It edits no frozen text. Item 9 records the owner's reading of the frozen J-7 and N-1 of ADR-D01 revision 6; the
  text of revision 6 stays as frozen at A7 ([A7 freeze](owner-decisions-2026-11-23-a7-freeze.md)).
- It accepts no ADR and moves no gate. ADR-D02's framework items and slot names freeze in round 2 with K2.
- It reopens neither DT-J1, nor the owner's answers of 17 and 22 October 2026, nor the 17 October repair-list
  exception.

## 1. Open research is ADR-D02's core (25 November 2026)

The owner's words, translated: "Remember: open research is the core of D02. Tell A4 that, as long as it stays inside
the framework, the freedom must be as high as possible."

The frame is the frozen ADR-D01 revision 6, J-8 included (research output never becomes a preview in a solving
session), and the human-solve boundary in solving sessions. Inside it, where two options differ in the researcher's
freedom, ADR-D02 takes the freer one. Five points follow without a pick and go into ADR-D02's text:
- every operation of the research module is a row a researcher can call from their own `.6cs` scripts;
- there is no fixed path and no forced order;
- views are placed freely;
- researchers name their own parts and records;
- seeds and depths are chosen freely within a declared budget.

## 2. The A4 picks (26 November 2026)

The owner's answer, translated: "A4: everything else as recommended; 9: Q3.b; 12c: set now (see note). Note: the
base target time: for a true expert, one restoration without Jumble has an upper bound of 3,000 hours, and this is
real time; in the puzzle with Jumble, there must be substantive progress on restoration theory under this ADR."

The recommended options taken:
- **1. Global layout in Jumble:** both the ring sheet (V1.A) and the Hopf rings (V1.B) become palette presets the
   researcher switches at any time; the ring sheet is Jumble's default. Each chart variant needs its own R5 admission
   and gate row. Until R5 admits a chart, the greybox keeps 600-cell-Full's projection chain (V1.C).
- **2. Raw view:** the antipodal second chart shows side by side, labelled "antipode".
- **3. Exposed faces:** a neutral matte by default; a contact-class tint is a mode the researcher can turn on with the
   domain colouring.
- **4. Height ramp:** one neutral-hue lightness ramp (2, 4, 6 and an overflow step), off by default.
- **5. Blocked cap:** a dark solid fill with a glyph that is not a slash; the two slash patterns stay with the slice's
   one-sided states.
- **6. C5's two values in the ν strip:** a five-position dial.
- **7. The word:** "Twist check".
- **8. Domain colours:** contact classes (E1.A), a proper colouring of the domain contact graph, replacing TH-02's one
   colour per domain. Measured at A4: four classes suffice at W-J record 300 and both deep exports (exact), one at the
   I_a@40 stop; the palette holds six classes and an overflow state.
- **10. Certificate-point glyphs:** they get their own gate row and the owner's acceptance, with the PHS.52 look-cost
    revision.
- **11. A round-4 moment, on record:** at the commit that reaches N = 0, the last domain's outline fades in place; it
    can be turned off and never shows at a check or a preview.
- **12. Track J's owner values** (A1-02), parts a and b:
    - seed pool 7401 to 7499: the scored batches take fixed seeds in order (J2: 7401 to 7406; J3: 7407 to 7421), and
      the researcher agent chooses seeds and depths freely from 7422 to 7499 for exploration;
    - runner-time budget per depth batch of three scrambles: depths 10 and 20 one hour each; 40, 100 and 300 twelve
      hours each; 1,000 and 3,000 twenty-four hours each; 24 hours of free exploration; about 110 hours in all. The
      researcher agent's API cost is separate and confirmed by the owner before each run.

## 3. Item 9: Q3.b, the owner's reading of J-7 and N-1

The kept checks list completed checks in release order with try numbers (Q3.a), and in addition each run of
consecutive superseded releases is folded into one row in its place: "not checked:" with those twists' glyphs and no
ΔN.

The round table split on it. The mathematician held that the row carries the solver's own twists and no field of the
cancelled job, and that it is written by the newer release, not by the cancel. The engineer advisor and the
interaction artist held that it breaks J-7 ("shows nothing of the cancelled job") and N-1 ("cancelling a job that is
no longer the latest request changes nothing on screen").

By taking Q3.b the owner reads J-7 and N-1 as the mathematician does:
- the folded row is written when the newer release is made, from the solver's own release record;
- it carries no field of a cancelled job;
- the cancel itself changes nothing on screen.

ADR-D02 states the row in these terms, with a test that the screen is the same before and after each cancel.

## 4. Item 12c: the base targets

**The base puzzle (Track B of the ADR-D03 solve test).** For a true expert, one full solve of 600-cell-Full without
jumbling has an upper bound of 3,000 hours, counted in real time. The estimated full solve time of the solve-test
plan is judged against this bar: the plan scored it only between 0.4 and the floor while no target existed. Reading
recorded by the ADR-D02 session: real time means the hours the solve actually takes, waits and session overhead
included, not keystroke time alone.

**The jumbled puzzle (Track J, ADR-D02's acceptance).** The target is not a time: the research module must yield
substantive progress on restoration theory under ADR-D02. The bars J0 to J4 stand; ADR-D02 states how a run shows
that progress against the frozen research data.
