# Owner decisions, 27 October 2026: the jumbling research handed to the engineering line

The owner gave these decisions in three chat messages in Chinese on 27 October 2026, after the
[freeze decisions](owner-decisions-2026-10-27-freeze.md) of the same day; this page records them in English.

## Decisions

1. **Hand over.** Once the freeze record is written, the jumbling research is handed over to the engineering line.
2. **A puzzle to be solved.** The jumbling line is designed as a puzzle to be solved. In the owner's words this amounts
   to researching the open methodology of restoring the complete puzzle, so that any scramble of depth 3000 can be
   restored. It is not designed by taking the answers the research found and reverse-engineering features from them.
3. **Research data only.** The handoff gives research data only, with no concrete engineering instructions.
4. **The answers already given stay; the rest assists the remaining research.** Asked whether the decisions that took
   research results into the product still hold, the owner kept them: among them the repair list at a cap the solver
   names ([17 October 2026](owner-decisions-2026-10-17-repair-pairs.md)) and the answers of
   [22 October 2026](owner-decisions-2026-10-22.md) (ENG-0J starts; the recommended DT-J1 options). What is not yet
   decided follows decision 2: it assists the research that remains open.

## What was handed over

- [`docs/progress/1.0/jumbling-handoff/research-data.md`](../../progress/1.0/jumbling-handoff/research-data.md): the
  research data at the freeze. It gives the definitions the records use, what is proved, what was computed at each
  scramble depth and where each method on record stopped, what is open, and where the records are and in which
  formats. It states no task, proposal or ranking for the engineering line.
- An entry dated 27 October 2026 on the engineering line's handoff page
  ([engineering.md](../../progress/1.0/jumbling-handoff/engineering.md)) points to it.
- The owner allowed one message to the engineering session: read the data page and this decision, and do nothing
  else.

## Integrator readings (the owner may change them)

- Data only is read as definitions, results with their status and sources, measurements, open questions and file
  formats. The data page has no "what the line does" column, no proposal and no order of approaches.
- The handoff and observation logs of 18 to 22 October keep their text: they are append-only and decide nothing. Their
  "what the line does with it" columns are the research's proposals of those dates, and this handoff adds none.
- "The answers already given" is read as the owner decisions in force on 27 October 2026, with the records they cite;
  "what is not yet decided" as every point those decisions leave open, among them the questions still marked open on
  the handoff pages.
- The freeze holds: the handoff ran no computation, and nothing in the research reopens.

## Effect

- The engineering line has the research data at the freeze. While the freeze holds, the research gives it no further
  input.
