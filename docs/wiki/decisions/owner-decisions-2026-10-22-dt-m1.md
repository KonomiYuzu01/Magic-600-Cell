# Owner decisions, 22 October 2026: the DT-M1 batch and a hold on design

The owner gave these answers in a chat message in Chinese; this page records them in English. The owner may revisit any of them.

1. **The DT-M1 batch: every recommended option, for now.** The questions and their options are in the outcome of [command-keyboard-model](../../progress/1.0/design-table/topics/command-keyboard-model.md).
   - Q1 (a): the grip-and-twist block bound by position, one fixed map of chords for the frequent commands, and prefix groups with tips, all under the palette.
   - Q2 (b): the other keys bind by position too, except undo and redo, which keep their office-software characters, with a conflict check whenever the layout changes.
   - Q3 (a): after a rebind, the grip keys are relative to the piece: M9's transported slot order, each slot with its transported frame.
   - Q4 (a): one undo key over a durable order of the solver's actions; an undo that another action has superseded is refused as stale.
   - Q5 (a): a commit or undo while a job runs goes ahead and cancels the jobs that read the replaced revision; file jobs finish.
   - Q6 (a): a commit typed while another is in flight is refused as busy and shown in place.
   - Q7 (a): four seat keys type the 48 jumble twists, pending experiment XM1.
   - Q8 (a): the first refusals of each category show their message in place.
   - Q9 (a): no key numbers; keys open with the learning path's steps.
   - Q10 (a): H-03 gains rows for a draft step, a preview scrub, an operation commit and a key opening or remap.
2. **The alternatives stay on record.** For every question, the options not chosen are kept as recorded alternatives, not discarded. The topic lists them beside each answer.
3. **The design line goes on hold** once its preliminary work is done, and **the round table goes on hold** once its current stage has run. The main session reads this as a hold from now on: DT-M1 ends the round table's current stage, and no further design-table topic opens (DT-J2 to DT-J4 included) until the owner resumes it.
