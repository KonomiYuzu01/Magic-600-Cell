600-Cell Grip Orbits

Grips are cell caps with a frame. A twist of a cap by a menu rotation moves every grip within the interaction angle, and the moved grips are added. This page shows how that grip set grows for each candidate twist menu, depth by depth.

Synthetic geometry. Float search with exact Q(√5) checks of sampled coincidences and of the closest pair. Leads for the theory work, not proofs.

Which grips twist

Stereographic projection of S³ from −n₀ into R³: the base pole n₀ is at the centre, and spheres about n₀ stay spheres. The faint sphere is the 46.8° interaction ball of n₀. Orbit points are a sample of the grips within 50° of n₀, coloured by BFS depth; lattice poles are drawn out to 120°. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom.

Growth by depth

Cumulative grips on S³ (log scale) for the selected menu, against every other menu with the same closure in grey. Click a grey line to select its menu. A dashed last segment and an open marker mean the level was cut off by the size or time budget, so its count is a lower bound.

Levels of the selected run

Grips, positions and orbit reps are cumulative; new grips, images and coinciding count that level only. Orbit reps are the K⁺-orbit representatives the search keeps; images are the twist images it computed, and coinciding images matched a known grip. Separation is the smallest angle between two distinct grip positions; it is left out for cut levels and for levels above 50,000 representatives.

Definitions and limits

Grip. A rotation P in SO(4) that carries the base pole n₀ and its frame to the grip's position P n₀. Frames are compared modulo the cap group A4, so the 600 lattice grips are the 600 poles.

Menu. A finite set of rotations fixing n₀, closed under inverses and A4-conjugation; it always contains A4. Grip P twists by P m P⁻¹. The realignment menus take one exact Q(√5) generator per class of the jumbling study; the plane menus rotate about the plane of n₀ and a face neighbour.

Closure. A twist of grip c moves every grip whose position lies within 46.8° of c (a ball standing in for the cap geometry, between the 44.48° and 49.12° shells). With "600 lattice grips" only the world-fixed lattice grips twist, as in the state contract; with "Every grip" each grip in the set twists by its own frame-conjugated menu.

Limits. The ball replaces the exact cap and piece geometry, and moved pieces change the outer shape, which this closure ignores. Growth within a budget does not prove a set infinite, and a closed run is checked exactly only on the sampled coincidences.
