# Restoring a jumbled state: theory and method (J3-R)

Status: **integrator's draft, 10 October 2026; not yet reviewed.** It extends the J3 theory draft ([`theory-draft.md`](theory-draft.md), items 5 and 6) with a model in which the jumbling of every piece is visible, and with a restoration method built on it. Labels follow the draft: **Proved**, **Computed** (exact, by the script named), **Lead** (sampled; suggests, proves nothing), **Open**. Scripts run from the repository root on top of the J1 reference (`research/jumbling/sim/`).

## 0. Problem

- **Input.** A configuration X reachable from solved under a jumbling menu (S4₀, I_a or I_b, or any menu that reaches what one of them reaches, theory draft Proposition 3.5). Only X is given: the poses of the pieces, which the solver sees. No journal.
- **Output.** A method the solver carries out: a sequence of admissible twists from X to solved, each chosen by the solver from what is visible. Tools may track, analyse, check and aid the view, and may run a setup search for a target the solver names; they never choose or output a solving move (human-solve boundary, owner decision of 2 October 2026).
- **Reverse engineering.** The method reads the jumbling structure off the state (which pieces were rotated together, by how much, around which pole) and undoes it, instead of replaying a history it does not have.
- **Complete.** For every reachable X the method reaches solved. Section 5 states what is proved, what is computed, what is a lead, and what is open.

Reversing the journal always works (theory draft, Proposition 1.2), but needs the journal.

## 1. The tree model

Notation: B is the quaternion algebra over F = Q(√5) with coordinates x = x₀ + x₁i + x₂j + x₃k, the coordinates of the state contract. O is the icosian ring, the Z[φ]-span of the 120 unit icosians 2I (standard coordinates: ±1, ½(±1, ±1, ±1, ±1) and the even permutations of ½(±φ, ±1, ±φ⁻¹, 0)). Γ(Λ) is the group generated by the transported menu elements of all 600 caps; every pose of every reachable configuration lies in Γ(Λ).

**T1 (proved): left and right factors.** Every rotation M of R⁴ with entries in F has the form M x = s · l x r̄ with l, r ∈ B and a scalar s ∈ F. The associate matrix A = (tr(E_ijᵀ M)/4), with E_ij the matrix of x ↦ e_i x ē_j, has rank one and equals l rᵀ up to a scalar, so l (a non-zero column of A) and r (a non-zero row) have entries in F. The classes [l], [r] ∈ B^×/F^× are determined by M. `trees.py` checks every extraction exactly (M e_b = s · l e_b r̄ for all four basis vectors with one scalar s).

**T2 (proved): two trees at 2.** 2 is inert in F, so F₂ = Q₂(√5) is the unramified quadratic extension of Q₂ with residue field F₄. B is ramified only at the two real places, so B ⊗ F₂ ≅ M₂(F₂), and O ⊗ Z₂[φ] is a maximal order there. PGL₂(F₂) acts on its Bruhat–Tits tree T, which is 5-regular (the projective line over F₄ has 5 points). Let v₀ be the vertex of O ⊗ Z₂[φ]. A pose γ = [l, r] has two tree positions, t_L(γ) = [l]·v₀ and t_R(γ) = [r]·v₀, and two heights d_L(γ) = d(v₀, t_L) and d_R(γ) = d(v₀, t_R).
- Distance formula: write x′ = 2⁻ᵏx with k maximal such that x′ ∈ O ⊗ Z₂[φ]; then d(v₀, [x]v₀) = v₂(N(x′)) (Cartan decomposition: x′ is primitive with elementary divisors 1 and 2^{v₂(N x′)}).
- Membership in O ⊗ Z₂[φ] is decided in a basis of four unit icosians whose Gram determinant under the reduced trace form is a 2-adic unit. Their span has unit discriminant, like the maximal order, so it is the whole local order.
- The branch of x (distance ≥ 1) is the neighbour of v₀ on the geodesic to [x]v₀. The five neighbours correspond to the five tetrahedral subgroups of A₅ = 2I/{±1}: the one fixing a neighbour w is Stab(v₀) ∩ Stab(w).

**T3 (proved, with two computed inputs): the lattice criterion.** For γ ∈ Γ(Λ): γ ∈ K⁺ if and only if d_L(γ) = d_R(γ) = 0.
- Computed inputs (`trees.py --check`): every transported menu element of every cap has entries in Z[φ][½]; all 7,200 elements of K⁺ are [u, w] with u, w unit icosians, so K⁺ is the rotation group of the 600-cell whose vertices are 2I.
- (⇒) K⁺ elements are [u, w] with units, which fix v₀ on both trees.
- (⇐) Suppose [l] and [r] fix v₀. A representative of each lies in F₂^×(O ⊗ Z₂[φ])^× (the normaliser of a maximal order of M₂(F₂)), so x ↦ s·l x r̄ maps O ⊗ Z₂[φ] onto itself; the scalar is a 2-adic unit because γ preserves the norm and the unit parts do. At an odd prime p, O ⊗ Z_p[φ] = Z_p[φ]⁴ (the index of Z[φ]⁴ in O is a power of 2), and γ has entries in Z[φ][½] ⊂ Z_p[φ] and is orthogonal, so it preserves that lattice too. Hence γ preserves O. It preserves the norm, so it permutes the 120 vectors of norm 1 of O, which are the unit icosians, the vertices of the 600-cell. A rotation permuting them lies in K⁺.

**T4 (computed): the menu elements on the trees.** For every cap c (`trees.py --check`, `results/trees.json`):
- S4_c: the 12 elements of A4_c have heights (0, 0); the 12 other elements have heights (1, 1), square into A4_c, and share one pair of branches (κ_L(c), κ_R(c)). Each is the inversion of the two edges {v₀, w_L(c)} and {v₀, w_R(c)}. Each of the 25 pairs (κ_L, κ_R) occurs on exactly 24 caps.
- I_a,c fixes w_L(c) on the left tree and v₀ on the right tree: its 48 elements outside A4_c have heights (2, 0). I_b,c is the mirror image: heights (0, 2), fixing v₀ on the left and w_R(c) on the right.

**T5 (proved from T4): how a twist changes heights.** A twist (c, h) maps the tree positions of every piece it moves by (t_L, t_R) ↦ (h_L t_L, h_R t_R).
- S4: an element of A4_c changes no height. An odd element changes d_L by −1 if t_L lies in the branch κ_L(c) (the geodesic from v₀ to t_L passes through w_L(c)) and by +1 otherwise; the same on the right with κ_R(c).
- I_a: d_R stays 0. A jumble element changes d_L by −2 when the geodesic from v₀ to t_L runs v₀, w_L(c), h⁻¹v₀, …; by 0 when it runs v₀, w_L(c), y with y ≠ h⁻¹v₀; by +2 otherwise (t_L = v₀ or a different branch). I_b mirrors this on the right.
- Consequence: under I_a every reachable pose has d_R = 0 and even d_L, under I_b d_L = 0 and even d_R, and under S4 d_L + d_R is even.

**T6 (proved): domains.** Two pieces are mutually aligned (γ_Q⁻¹γ_P ∈ K⁺, so their posed regions lie in one rotated copy of the chamber tiling) if and only if they have the same tree positions (t_L, t_R) (T3 applied to γ_Q⁻¹γ_P ∈ Γ(Λ)). A **domain** is such a class, a left coset γK⁺. The lattice is the domain (v₀, v₀).
- Every quantity here is a function of the poses, so it is visible: which domain a piece belongs to, how deep it is (d_L, d_R), and towards which branch.
- The domains are few. At height 1 in each tree (S4) there are 25; at height 2 on one tree (I_a) there are 20, one per pair (w, y) with y a neighbour of w other than v₀.

**T7 (proved): lower bounds.** Under S4 a piece at heights (d_L, d_R) must still be moved by at least max(d_L, d_R) odd twists before it is on the lattice; under I_a by at least d_L/2 jumble twists. The total height H(X) = Σ_P (d_L + d_R) is 0 exactly on the lattice L, and a single jumble twist changes it by at most 2 per piece it moves.

**Lead (exact states): heights stay small.** Along the W-J fixtures (`trees.py --fixtures`, results in `results/trees-fixtures.json`), almost every off-lattice piece is one jumble twist deep: under S4 at t = 640, 81,229 of the 117,498 off-lattice pieces are at (1, 1), and the largest heights are (2, 4) and (3, 1); under I_a at t = 150, 118,803 of 120,214 are at height 2 and the rest at 4.
