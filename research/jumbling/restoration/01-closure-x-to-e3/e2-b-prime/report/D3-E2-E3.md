## How the word was found, and what each step read

Every constructor read `E2-table.json`, the word files on its command line and the template shapes. None read the
reachability certificate, the generating word or any old suffix, and no other state replaced E2.

The simulator loader also reads the model assets (`assets/model.npz`, `assets/primitives.npz`) and the fixture file
`fixtures/wj-I_a.json`, whose menu record defines the letters. It builds a scratch state by replaying the fixture's
first 40 scramble records, then replaces the whole pose table with E2's and checks the digest. Segments 1 and 2 used
the same loader. An auditor reran all four constructors with a blank state carrying the same menu: the fixture file
was never opened, and every output was identical apart from timing (`audit/words/nofix-compare.json`; file traces in
`audit/words/trace/`).

1. `singleP.py` (B3): the single letters that put B' on K are 24, (237, i) and (257, i) for i in 13, 17, 19, 34 to 42.
   Each parks its whole cap, 3,097 pieces (segment-2 `B3/typeS1-237.jsonl` and `B3/typeS1-257.jsonl`; an auditor
   checked all 472 non-identity letters of the 8 grips B' blocks, admissible or not, `audit/shrink/single.json`).
2. `shrink.py`: iterated conjugation from R = (237,13), described in the next section. Five levels, 222 s.
3. At level 4, 55 letters satisfy the finishing condition; F = (162,1) is the least.
4. `constructS.py`, type S: U = P4 Q0 F P4^-1 with Q0 an 8-letter word of the letters whose caps avoid T. This was the
   first success: 71 twists, every twist admissible, off 3 -> 0, collateral 0, 156,104 lattice pieces at their exact
   pose (`D3/typeS/`).
   - Q0 came out letter for letter equal to V3 below, although the two certificates differ, so the type-S word is
     P4 V3 F P4^-1.
   - The script checks F through three premises: F(BH) inside T, N outside T, and |N| = |GH|. Together these imply that F
     moves all of L out of T only when |GH| = |L|, that is, when Bad(P4) holds no hole of E2. That holds here
     (|GH| = 3). The level-4 record also checks it directly: F's cap holds all of L_4 and moves it out.
5. `closure2.py`, the composable form used in segments 1 and 2: W = P4 F P4^-1, vacated by a SAFE-ALL word V3 at E2.
   This is the delivered word. Its input `w3-P4-162-1.json` and `P4.json` are level 4's P and its least finishing
   letter, copied from `shrink-237-13.jsonl`.

The shape is new: the iterated conjugation is not one of the template families of A-B-closure.md. What makes the
delivered word trustworthy is the certificate recorded at E2 before any twist (below) and the exact replay, not the
family.

## The new template: iterated conjugation shrinks the bubble

The earlier report on B' (segment-2 package, "B' construction attempts at E2") found the obstacle for a single-letter
P. After P = (237,13), the only SAFE-ALL cap holding a chamber of B' is grip 91's. It holds 332 bad holes (holes inside
Bad(P^-1)), and each of its 11 letters takes 131 or 332 of them out of Bad(P^-1). So no single SAFE-ALL finishing
letter F exists there.

Notation at a level k, all computed from E2 by applying P(k) to it:
- Y_k = E2 · P(k), the state after P(k);
- T_k = Bad(P(k)^-1), a word-only chamber ghost;
- BH_k, the holes of Y_k inside T_k (the bad holes);
- L_k, the chambers of B', which P(k) puts on K inside T_k.

The step from level k to level k+1 takes a K+ letter x_k that is SAFE-ALL at Y_k and moves all of L_k out of T_k. It
does not move any piece into a chamber of T_k whose ghost straddles a cut under P(k)^-1. Then P(k+1) = P(k) x_k
P(k)^-1 is admissible at E2 and puts B' on K. The reasons:
- At Y_k the only lattice pieces in T_k are B'. P(k)^-1 reverses an admissible word, so it is admissible from Y_k.
  Every lattice piece of E2 that P(k) did not park retraces under it to its E2 chamber on K, so its chamber at Y_k is
  in Good(P(k)^-1), while B' retraces off K. So T_k consists of L_k and the bad holes BH_k.
- After P(k) x_k, the pieces P(k) parked are where they were at Y_k, because x_k's cap holds no off piece. So under
  P(k)^-1 they follow the same frame ghost as from Y_k and return to their E2 chambers on K.
- Every other piece is a lattice content at Y_k · x_k. If x_k does not move it into T_k, it sits in a chamber of
  Good(P(k)^-1), by the first point and because x_k moves L_k out of T_k. If x_k moves it into T_k, it sits in a chamber
  whose ghost does not straddle (no kill violation), so it ends off K. So no twist of P(k)^-1 is blocked.
- B' sits in x_k(L_k), outside T_k, so it ends on K.
- The pieces P(k+1) parks are exactly the lattice pieces x_k moves into T_k. x_k permutes its cap, so it moves as many
  chambers into T_k as out of it: the 3 of L_k and the bh_out bad holes. The entering pieces are these minus the holes
  from outside T_k that x_k moves in. No step letter does that on this instance, because the good holes (the 3 holes of
  E2 at every level) lie in no SAFE-ALL cap holding a chamber of L_k. So the count is 3 + bh_out (`enter_pieces` and
  `bh_out` in `shrink-237-13.jsonl`; `audit/math/step-L*.json`).

The argument uses only "no kill violation", not `T_straddle` = 0.

This explains each step. The replay below is what checks it.

## Why the second half is legal

The argument restates segment 2's for W:
- points 1 and 2 split segment 2's point 2, and, like its point 1, treat the pieces restored in segments 1 and 2 as
  ordinary lattice content;
- point 3 is segment 2's point 3 with B' as the target and no protected piece;
- point 4 is unchanged;
- point 5 is simpler, because no off piece remains after W.

1. Bad(W) depends only on the word. It covers every lattice content that W would take off or make straddle.
2. V3 vacates Bad(W) before W starts (certificate above; checked after V3). So no lattice piece of E2 · V3 is in
   Bad(W).
3. The only off pieces at E2 · V3 are B'. V3 is SAFE-ALL, so they are at their E2 pose. The frame ghost follows them
   exactly through W: it kills a content at the first twist whose cut it straddles. B' is alive at the end (on K, 3 of
   3), so B' never straddles in W.
4. Hence every twist of W is admissible from E2 · V3, and the end of W is the ghost's prediction: B' on K, every
   lattice piece of E2 · V3 on K.
5. V3^-1 then acts on a state with every piece on K. Every grip is SAFE-ALL there (no off piece, no straddle), so every
   letter of V3^-1 is admissible. K+ letters keep every piece on K.

The exact replay checks each of these at every prefix.

## B' diagnosis, updated

The exclusions in the segment-2 package are unchanged and still stand for the families named there. The word found here
lies in both families that package listed as not excluded:
- "other P, such as a long restoring word that parks few pieces": P4 has 31 letters and parks 10;
- "these P with a longer Q'": W = P4 F P4^-1 begins with R x0 and ends with x0^-1 R^-1, so W = R Q' R^-1 with
  Q' = x0 h x0^-1 of 61 letters. Here h contains R and R^-1, which are not K+ letters, so W lies outside the excluded
  x h x^-1 family, whose h used only letters whose caps avoid T.

The bubble witness constrains only single SAFE-ALL finishing letters after P = (237,13). It does not obstruct that P
with a longer word. Iterated conjugation removes the bubble in four steps on this instance.
