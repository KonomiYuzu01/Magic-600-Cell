# D. A clean net restoration on X: the 17-block (Claude, 12 October 2026)

Evidence kind: exact simulator replay, exact synthetic geometry and exact chamber ghosts on isolated public test data.
No native-host, Windows/DirectX or performance evidence. This is one instance on one input. It does not claim a general
solution of E.

## Result

At X = X-20261012-1 (digest `f13c168e…dc0d7`, 107 off pieces in blocks of 87, 17 and 3), the native word

    V · P · Q · P^-1 · V^-1        (541 + 10 + 10 + 10 + 541 = 1,112 twists)

meets all four parts of the success condition:

1. The 17-block (smallest piece 9117) ends on the lattice: restored 17.
2. Every lattice piece of X ends on the lattice, at its exact start pose: collateral 0. Comparing the start and end
   pose tables entry by entry, exactly 17 pieces have a different exact pose: the 17 pieces of the block.
3. The other 90 off pieces (the 87-block and B') end at their exact start poses: pose_changed 0
   (`off-pieces-start-end.json` lists all 107 matrices at the start and at the end).
4. Zero collateral at the end. Stage by stage, the only lattice pieces of X that are ever off at a stage end are
   the 198 parked pieces C, after P and after Q. They are the same 198 pieces at both ends, and P^-1 returns them all.

End digest `903997457de665c2846e00ba62b71c3d3ccb7df79c78391f5a1b9f1757afc4e8`; off 107 -> 90.

The constructor read only `X-table.json`. It never read the reachability certificate or any generating word, and
it did not replay an old success suffix. The literal old window 36568 is blocked at X (C-X.md, counterexample 1).

## How it was found (current state only)

1. Q search (`dcomm2.py`). 60,588 candidates Q = [A, [B, X]] for the 17-block met the block conditions G2. The ghost
   scored each one: T on the lattice, the other off pieces exact, and Bad(Q). The minimum |Bad(Q)| is 198, more
   than the 107 holes, so by the counting identity no SAFE-ALL conjugation of Q alone can be clean at X.
2. Lens shape (`triples.py`). For the chosen Q, Bad(Q) is exactly the triple lens 56 ∩ 95 ∩ 107 (198 chambers, all
   occupied at X), with 95 = c (X's grip) and 107 = the pole X^-1 n_43 (e = 43, B's grip).
3. Parking (`parksearch.py`, rule G4). For 21 Q's of the owner's shape, 4,378 parking words P were scored by the
   ghost of W = P Q P^-1. Twenty-three of them, spread over 6 Q's, give T on the lattice, the other off pieces exact
   and |Bad(W)| = 87.
4. Vacating and replay (`finishw.py`). Bad(W) has 87 chambers, all occupied at X, and X has 107 holes. N1 holds (no
   fixed bad chamber is occupied) and N2 holds (no orbit deficit), with 570 SAFE-ALL grips. The peeling certificate
   gave V (541 letters). After V no chamber of Bad(W) holds a lattice piece. Then W and V^-1 were replayed.

## The window at X, class by class (`window-X.json`, conjclose.py on the state after V)

The classes are fixed at s, the state after V:
- T: the 17-block;
- Prot: the 90 other off pieces;
- C: the lattice pieces that P takes off (198);
- L: all other lattice pieces (176,815).

- P selects L at all 10 letters and C at 7 letters. It never selects T or Prot. It parks C, 198 pieces.
- Q selects T at 4 letters (its letters 1, 6, 8 and 9: A, A^-1, the second B and the second X^-1) and L at all 10. It never selects Prot or C. Its net-moved
  set is M = T exactly. T lands in 17 chambers that were holes at s, which V had vacated.
- P^-1 selects M (now on the lattice) at 6 of its 10 letters, where the mirror letters of P selected none of M.
  Outside M, every P^-1 letter selects exactly what its mirror P letter selected (F2). Every piece outside M returns
  to its exact pose at s (F1, 177,103 pieces). The ghost of M from its landing equals the real end, and M ends on K
  (F3).
- Allowed landing for Q: Good(P^-1). Bad(P^-1) is the 198 holes P creates (all clean, all ending off). No chamber of
  Bad(P^-1) is occupied after P or after Q.

This has the same structure as window 36568 at 36563 (A-B-closure.md, section A), for a different block, different
grips and a different lens. The one change is that the old window's landing chambers were holes at s, while at X
they and the rest of Bad(W) were all occupied, and V vacates them first.

## Not shown, and the limits of the search

- Only the 17-block is restored. The 87-block and B' stay off at their exact poses. A Q search for the 87-block at X
  (`dcomm2.py`, block 0) is running and has no result yet. For B', the single-letter conjugate failed
  (C-X.md, counterexample 2).
- G3 and G4 are computed on these instances, not proved for the family.
- V exists here because N1 and N2 hold and the peeling certificate succeeded. N1 and N2 are necessary, not
  sufficient. With fewer holes than |Bad(W)| = 87 this route cannot work.
- Search scope for this result:
  - 21 owner-shaped Q's (distinct (e, c)) out of the 60,588 G2 candidates;
  - parking words of the G4 shape only;
  - one of the 23 parking words with |Bad(W)| = 87 carried to replay (for the third of the 21 Q's); the other
    22 are untried.
