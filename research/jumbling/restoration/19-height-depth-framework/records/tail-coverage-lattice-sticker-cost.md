## Appending it to the new tail family

The tail theorem applies to every valid state with table00097's exact off data and source SAFE profile. For the **reachable members** of that family, every actual endpoint produced by the legal tail construction is still reachable, regardless of which allowed preparations or hole arrangements were chosen. The all-lattice endpoint therefore satisfies premise2, and the existing lattice restorer applies.

Consequently a reachable input in that family has a sticker-solving word of length at most B_tail+20892696, where B_tail is the explicit6775digit ten-stage bound in the frozen completion manifest. The tail-stage count and profile proof do not supply an exact canonical-pose solution: TheoremD's warning at7164 remains relevant, because rotations invisible to all sticker labels may remain. No arbitrary unreachable lattice input is included.

The correction library also gives a coarse explicit height ceiling. Therefore its maximum height is at most2*floor(J/2), with the same bound for its inverse. Exact menu classification of the fixed library gives maximum2048over its36macros. This is a coarse derived bound, not a measured prefix maximum; the parent is separately checking tighter ghost heights.
