## The final finite-family milestone

For a full reference pose table `Y`, the family `Gamma(Y)` has a type-preserving
bijection `pi` and right gauges `t_p` in the finite retained group `K+`, with

```text
t_p(home_region(p)) = home_region(pi(p))
Z_p = Y_pi(p) t_p.
```

A finite exact matching problem recognizes this family from the current state.
Corresponding occupied regions and complete legal-word languages agree. The
same certificate transfers along each legal prefix, including all collateral
effects. This is a mathematical finite recognition algorithm; a general practical
matching implementation was not built.

## Upper bounds and complete branches

For fixed maximum macro length `L`, typed lexicographic descent is equivalent
to descent of `R_L = sum(q_t product_(s>t)(1+L N_s))` on these bounded
transitions. If every admitted nonterminal state has a legal selected macro
which stays admitted and strictly decreases that rank, the high phase costs at
most `L R_L(Z0)` and has peak at most `2 R_L(Z0)+2+2L`. In terms of input
maximum height H, the conditional cost is

```text
max(H-2,0)/2 * (product_t(1+L N_t) - 1).
```

This is a bounded-difference argument, not an encoding of arbitrary unbounded
vectors. The controlled-cost extension replaces fixed L with
`length <= a sum(q_t)+b` and provides an explicit recursive `C_36` bound.
Neither theorem proves that the needed macro exists at every reachable state.
Neither closes a height-at-most-2 endpoint by itself.

There is a complete branch on a different, precisely recognized infinite
domain. For the guarded pump-prefix reference family, including incomplete
prefixes, exact reference restoration costs at most `H/2+68`. On its regional
fibres, **with a reachability promise**, geometric return reaches a reachable
lattice state and the existing sticker theorem closes the goal in at most
`H/2+20892764` letters. The no-overshoot result proves full-state peak exactly H
on this domain (`H >= 3888`), using the checked seed peak 30 and non-target
suffix ceiling 32. It does not apply to every high state.

The other complete low-height branch starts at the specified D1 entry geometry
and SAFE profile. Eleven preserved profile stages lead from 1,193 off pieces
to lattice, including the ten-stage 1,082-piece suffix. With `N=177120`,

```text
B10 = 2 binom(N,198) + binom(N,111) + binom(N,86) + 740
    + sum_(n in {673,389,241,216,185,60}) binom(N,n)
    + 6 binom(N,3097)
B11 = B10 + binom(N,111) + 59
C   = 20892696
T   = B11 + C.
```

The preparations are reconstructed at the actual endpoint; matching profiles
do not license reuse of a stored literal word. B11 is a theoretical 6,775-digit
bound. No full expansion/replay of that bound or practical runtime is claimed.
The [lattice height audit](records/lattice-library-height-independent-review.md)
sharpens the older coarse 2,048 ceiling to 2, including correction inverses.

For fixed k, finite legal enumeration tests whether a promised-reachable input
is within k letters of lattice, the specified D1 profile, or the regional high
domain. On precisely that admitted domain `D[k]`, the composed bounds are

```text
U(h,k) = max(k+C, k+T, h/2+2k+20892764)
E(h,k) = max(h+2k, 2).
```

Rejection means `OUTSIDE_CERTIFIED_DOMAIN(k)`, not unreachable or unsolvable.
Finite geometric admission is total on the generated exact pose universe;
successful sticker synthesis retains the reachability promise. An unbounded
increase of k recovers an eventual search on reachable states, not a new
quantitative global bound.
