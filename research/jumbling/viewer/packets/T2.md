## 1. Goal and acceptance

Packet T1 (committed) added `V/export_tutorial.py`, the `?shot=` mode of `V/full.js` and `V/full.html`, and
`V/capture_tutorial.mjs`. The integrator captured seed 7001 step 0 (cap 72, 3,097 moved pieces) and found three
problems. Fix them in the shot mode only; the page without `?shot` must stay exactly as it is, and `V/check_full.mjs`
must keep passing.

1. **Occlusion.** With `emph=moved`, the dimmed stickers are drawn opaque, so they hide the moved pieces behind
   them: the local view shows only shards of the 3,097 moved pieces, and the global view shows none of them.
   Required: the emphasised stickers are drawn opaque and complete; every other sticker is a faint translucent ghost
   (about 0.12 alpha, no depth write, drawn after the opaque pass) that never hides an emphasised sticker. A
   two-pass draw of the same instanced mesh with a uniform that selects the class is fine.
2. **Emphasis classes.** `emph=moved` (default): the step's moved pieces. New `emph=off`: every piece off the
   lattice (from the state's `pose_lattice`). `emph=none`: everything opaque, as now.
3. **Orientation and framing.**
   - New `cap=<0..599>` (default: the first cap of `tutorial.json` `caps`, or none when the list is empty).
   - When a cap is set, both views rotate the 4D view (Q) so that this cap is the one nearest the 4D eye of the
     current projection (perspective `d4 = 1.18`, or stereographic from the opposite pole) and its projected centre
     is at the centre of the image. Check which sign of w is near the eye in the page's projection code; do not
     guess.
   - `view=global` frames the whole drawn puzzle so that it fills about 85% of the shorter image side.
   - `view=local` frames the cap and its four face neighbours (the nearest cell centres) so that they fill about
     80% of the shorter side. Frame the cells' fixed positions (unposed facet geometry), not the current poses of
     their home pieces, so pieces moved elsewhere do not pull the camera away.
   - With no cap, the global view keeps the page's default rotation, framed as above; `view=local` without a cap is
     an error.
4. `V/capture_tutorial.mjs` passes `cap` through from the list entries (optional field) and records it in the
   manifest. `--check` must still compare PNG hashes.
5. Update the "Tutorial screenshots" section of `V/README.md` for the new parameters, briefly.

Acceptance (the contract's check): `V/test_export_tutorial.py` passes, including its `node --check` of `V/full.js`
and `V/capture_tutorial.mjs`. Add to it a pure computation test of the orientation if you factor that math into a

## 2. Actual problem and reproduction

`python3 -I -B V/export_tutorial.py R/d0-s7001/run --step 0 --when before --out <new dir>`, then a capture with
`view=global, emph=moved` shows a uniformly pale puzzle; `view=local` shows scattered fragments of the moved pieces
among pale opaque stickers. The fragment shader of T1 mixes non-moved stickers toward the background
(`V/full.js`, `uEmphasizeMoved`) but keeps them opaque with depth writes. `frameLocal` fits the posed stickers of
the cap's facet and its neighbours.
