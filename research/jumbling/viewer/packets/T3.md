## 1. Goal and acceptance

The owner chose the stereographic local view (`view=local&proj=stereo`, cap centred, emphasised pieces opaque) as
the main tutorial figure, with fewer ghosts, and the perspective view for whole-puzzle overviews (22 October 2026).
Two changes in the shot mode only; the page without `?shot` must stay exactly as it is, and `V/check_full.mjs` must
keep passing.

1. **Sparse ghosts.** In the stereographic local view, the translucent ghosts of all 600 cells pile up into a dense
   coloured background around the cap. Add `ghost=near|all|none`:
   - `near` (default for `view=local`): draw a ghost only for a sticker whose posed 4D position lies within an angle
     θ of the cap's centre. Choose θ so that the cap and every cell sharing at least a vertex with it are inside, and
     no cell beyond them; state the value and how you derived it from the cell centres.
   - `all` (default for `view=global`): as now. `none`: no ghosts.
   - Lower the ghost alpha from 0.12 to 0.08.
   - Emphasised pieces are always drawn opaque wherever they are.
2. **Stereographic framing.** `view=global&proj=stereo` currently gives an empty image: stickers near the projection
   pole (the antipode of the cap) push the camera distance towards infinity. Frame the global stereographic view by
   the stickers within 90° of the cap centre only (all stickers are still drawn). The local view keeps framing the cap
   and its four face neighbours.
3. `V/capture_tutorial.mjs` passes `ghost` through (optional field) and records it in the manifest. Update the
   "Tutorial screenshots" section of `V/README.md` briefly.

Acceptance (the contract's check): `V/test_export_tutorial.py` passes, including its `node --check` runs. Add a pure
computation test for θ (all 56 cells touching a cap, and only those, are within θ, for every cap) if you can run that
math in node or Python without a browser. Your sandbox cannot start Chromium; the integrator runs the captures and
`V/check_full.mjs`.

Out of scope: annotations, captions, colours (provisional; the owner decides G1 to G6), the exporter, other renderers,
performance.
